# Monthly audit of synthetic data

The bundled demo contains 13 invented transactions in three invented accounts.
It has no connection to a bank. `finance --demo monthly YYYY-MM` produces this
result for the month generated by the demo provider, after sync/classification:

| Currency | Expense total | Included records | Excluded records |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ILS | 419.90 | 4 | 8 |
| USD | 10.00 | 1 | 0 |

The ILS total is 250.00 + 120.00 + 69.90 - 20.00 (refund).
Excluded: two internal transfers, one investment, one card settlement linked to
the imported card account, one income, one technical movement, one pending
transaction, and one reversed transaction. Every bucket also reports its signed
source amount and its contribution to expenses. Currencies are never summed.

Limits that need review when connecting a real provider:

- The default monthly timezone is Asia/Jerusalem; `--timezone` can change it.
- An explicit normalized movement type supplies transfer/investment evidence.
  Equal-and-opposite amounts alone are not proof of an internal transfer.
- A card settlement is excluded only with an explicit card-account link,
  completed bootstrap, and imported posted card records in the selected month.
  Statement periods spanning months and partial provider history need the real
  provider's coverage/statement contract. Unmatched settlements remain visible
  as `unmatched_card_settlement`, counted conservatively if they are debits.
- Refunds reduce expenses in the month received. A credit explicitly linked to
  an already reversed debit is excluded instead of subtracting twice.
- ID changes need the normalized pending transaction link. Pending records
  without such a link remain pending and are excluded from expense totals.
- ID-less duplicates preserve multiplicity using a deterministic occurrence
  ordinal. Changed ID-less payloads need provider-specific reconciliation;
  indistinguishable separate purchases cannot safely be guessed away.
- Classification is required before totals are calculated. Manual rules take
  priority over merchant rules; explicit manual flags override derived flags.

`tests/test_finance_logic.py` verifies the audit, priority/backfill, reversals,
unmatched settlements, month boundaries and exact decimal totals.
